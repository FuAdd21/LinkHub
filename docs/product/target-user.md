# LinkHub Target Users

## Primary Persona: Career Builder

People who are actively building their careers online and need a professional presence.

---

## Persona 1: The Developer

**Example**: Fuad, 24, Full-Stack Developer in Addis Ababa

**Online presence**:
- GitHub (repositories, contributions)
- LinkedIn (professional network)
- Portfolio website
- YouTube (tutorials)
- Telegram (community)
- WhatsApp (contact)

**Core need**: "I want to send one link that shows my projects, GitHub, and makes it easy for recruiters or clients to contact me."

**Current pain**: Sends 3-4 different links depending on who's asking. Portfolio is outdated. GitHub doesn't show his best work clearly.

**LinkHub value**: One URL with featured projects, GitHub stats, LinkedIn, and a "Hire me" CTA.

---

## Persona 2: The Design Student

**Example**: Sara, 22, Graphic Design student in Nairobi

**Online presence**:
- Behance portfolio
- Instagram (visual work)
- Dribbble (case studies)
- LinkedIn
- CV (PDF)

**Core need**: "I need a professional-looking page to share with potential employers during my job search."

**Current pain**: Her Behance alone doesn't show her full range. Interviewers always ask for LinkedIn, CV, and Instagram separately.

**LinkHub value**: One URL with featured case studies, credentials (degree), social presence, and "Download CV" CTA.

---

## Persona 3: The Freelancer

**Example**: Ahmed, 28, Freelance videographer in Dubai

**Online presence**:
- YouTube (reel)
- Instagram (portfolio)
- WhatsApp Business
- Upwork profile
- TikTok (behind-the-scenes)

**Core need**: "Potential clients find me on Instagram but need to see my rates and book me."

**Current pain**: Uses Linktree but it's just a list of links — doesn't communicate his services or work quality.

**LinkHub value**: One URL with featured video projects, social proof (follower counts), service description, and "Book a call" CTA.

---

## Persona 4: The Content Creator

**Example**: Liya, 26, Tech content creator in Lagos

**Online presence**:
- YouTube (main platform)
- Twitter/X (engagement)
- LinkedIn (professional)
- Telegram (community)
- Newsletter

**Core need**: "I need my audience to find all my platforms easily and join my community."

**Current pain**: Bio link on Instagram gets outdated. Analytics from Linktree don't tell her what visitors actually want.

**LinkHub value**: One URL with content showcase, social platforms, community links, and engagement analytics that show "what visitors care about."

---

## Who We Are NOT Building For

| Audience | Why Not |
|----------|---------|
| Large businesses | They need full websites, not link pages |
| Influencers seeking monetization | Beacons/Linktree already dominate this |
| Musicians/artists seeking streaming links | Specialized tools (Linkfire) serve them better |
| E-commerce sellers | Shopify/Gumroad serve them better |

## Geographic Consideration

LinkHub should work well for:
- Mobile-first users (majority of African internet users)
- Low-bandwidth connections
- Telegram/WhatsApp-heavy communication cultures
- International freelancers seeking global clients

This isn't a "made for Africa" product — it's a globally useful product that doesn't ignore these realities the way many Western-first tools do.
