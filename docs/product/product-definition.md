# LinkHub Product Definition

## Core Problem

People building careers online have their professional identity scattered across portfolios, GitHub, LinkedIn, social platforms, CVs, projects, and messaging platforms.

When someone asks "Where can I learn more about you?", they have no single answer.

## Core Solution

LinkHub gives them **one professional identity URL** containing the most important parts of their online presence — their work, their proof, their social presence, and their contact options.

## What LinkHub Is NOT

- Not another Linktree clone competing on "more links"
- Not an influencer/creator monetization platform
- Not a full website builder or portfolio CMS
- Not an AI-powered anything (yet)

## Primary User

Students, developers, designers, freelancers, and other career-focused individuals who need to present themselves professionally online.

### Example Users

| User | Primary Need |
|------|-------------|
| **Ethiopian freelancer** | Portfolio + GitHub + Upwork + WhatsApp + CV in one link |
| **Computer science student** | Projects + Resume + GitHub + LinkedIn for job applications |
| **UI/UX designer** | Behance + Dribbble + Case studies + Contact |
| **Content creator** | YouTube + Social profiles + Contact + Booking |

## Core Job

> When someone asks "What do you do?" or "Where can I see your work?", the user shares **one URL**.

That URL should communicate:
1. **Who they are** — name, role, positioning
2. **What they've done** — projects, portfolio, proof of work
3. **Where to find them** — social presence
4. **How to reach them** — contact, CTA

## Core Success Event

A visitor performs a **meaningful action** after viewing a profile:

- Portfolio visit
- Project view
- CV download
- Contact initiation
- Social follow
- External link click
- Booking/hire action

## Product Positioning

### Instead of:
> "All your links. One beautiful page."

### We say:
> **"Your work deserves more than a list of links."**

Then:
> **Create one professional profile for your portfolio, projects, social presence, and contact information.**

## What Makes LinkHub Different

| Generic link-in-bio | LinkHub |
|---------------------|---------|
| Links list | Professional identity |
| View count | Visitor engagement story |
| Theme colors | Purpose-driven templates |
| "Add a link" | "Show your work" |
| Vanity metrics | Actionable insights |

## Non-Goals (For Now)

- Custom domains
- Team/organization accounts
- E-commerce / monetization
- AI-generated content
- Paid subscription tiers
- Mobile app

These may come later, only after validating the core with real users.
